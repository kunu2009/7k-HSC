1. **Unipolarity** - Single superpower (USA after 1991)
2. **Multipolarity** - Multiple centers of power (China, India, EU)

### Important Distinguish Between Topics
- Soft Power vs Hard Power
- Unipolarity vs Multipolarity
- Globalization vs Localization
- E-Governance vs Good Governance

### Key Formulas

#### Utility Analysis
- **MU = ΔTU / ΔQ**
- Consumer Equilibrium: **MUx/Px = MUy/Py = MUm**

#### Elasticity of Demand
- **Ed = (% Change in Quantity) / (% Change in Price)**
- **Ed = (ΔQ/Q) × (P/ΔP)**

#### Index Numbers
- **Simple Aggregative: P₁/P₀ × 100**
- **Weighted Aggregative: Σ(P₁Q₀)/Σ(P₀Q₀) × 100**

#### National Income
- **GDP = C + I + G + (X-M)**
- **NDP = GDP - Depreciation**
- **NNP = GNP - Depreciation**
