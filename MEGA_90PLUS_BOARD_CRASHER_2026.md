### 🟣 ECONOMICS (90+ Target)

**Paper Pattern (80 Marks)**:
| Section | Marks | Type |
|---------|-------|------|
| MCQs | 20 | 20 × 1 mark |
| Short Answers | 32 | Reasons, Distinguish |
| Long Answers | 16 | 2 × 8 marks |
| Diagrams | 12 | Curves with explanation |

**Guaranteed Scoring Areas**:
1. **Elasticity**: All 5 types with formulas
2. **Demand/Supply Curves**: Movement vs Shift
3. **National Income Methods**: Formulae
4. **Market Structures**: Comparison table

**Formulas to Memorize**:
```
Price Elasticity (Ed) = % Change in Qty Demanded / % Change in Price
Income Elasticity (Ey) = % Change in Qty Demanded / % Change in Income
Cross Elasticity (Ec) = % Change in Qty of A / % Change in Price of B

GDP = C + I + G + (X-M)
GNP = GDP + Net Factor Income from Abroad
NNP = GNP - Depreciation
NI = NNP at Factor Cost
```

## 🆘 EMERGENCY PROTOCOLS

### "I'm Behind Schedule!"
1. Don't panic - adapt the plan
2. Focus on HIGH-WEIGHTAGE chapters only
3. Skip minor topics completely
4. Use 70-30 rule: 70% important, 30% revision

### "I Can't Remember Anything!"
1. Switch to active recall (test yourself)
2. Use spaced repetition
3. Teach the concept to someone
4. Write it down 3 times

### "Exam is Tomorrow & I'm Not Ready!"
1. Stop new topics - only revision
2. Read summaries only
3. Do 1 PYQ paper (don't check answers)
4. Sleep by 10 PM - Brain consolidates in sleep

### "I Failed a Paper!"
1. It's NOT over - you have more exams
2. Don't let it affect next exam
3. Analyze what went wrong
4. Move on with full energy

### Answer Writing Tips:

1. **MCQs** (20 marks):
   - Don't spend >1 min per MCQ
   - Mark uncertain ones, come back later
   - Elimination technique

2. **Short Answers** (4-5 marks):
   - 4-5 points with explanation
   - Underline key terms
   - Keep it precise

3. **Long Answers** (8-10 marks):
   - Introduction (1 mark)
   - Body with subheadings (6-7 marks)
   - Conclusion (1-2 marks)
   - Diagram if applicable (+bonus)

4. **Map Work**:
   - Use pencil first, then pen
   - Label clearly
   - Color if allowed
